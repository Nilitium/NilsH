## d\carb Future Economy Forum

Selected sessions from the [d\carb future economy forum](https://www.decarb.world), which I founded in 2020. The forum convenes leading economists, practitioners, and policymakers around green innovation, carbon dioxide removal, and the political economy of net zero.
