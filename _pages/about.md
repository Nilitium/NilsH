I am an economist and strategist working at the intersection of **climate policy, green public procurement, and evidence-based institutional change**. My research focuses on green innovation policy, carbon dioxide removal, and the political economy of the low-carbon transition — with a particular emphasis on randomized controlled trials and quasi-experimental methods.

I am a PhD candidate at the [Berlin School of Economics](https://www.berlin-school-of-economics.org/) (intake <4%, fully funded), with research stays at **MIT Sloan School of Management**, the **LSE Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change**, and **Imperial College London**. My PhD committee includes [Prof. Marion Dumas](https://mariondumas.github.io/) (LSE), [Prof. Ottmar Edenhofer](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/members/edenh) (TU Berlin), and [Prof. Jan Steckel](https://www.b-tu.de/en/fg-klimapolitik/team/prof-dr-jan-steckel).

Since August 2024, I lead the **climate neutrality agenda** at the Office of the President of the University of Zurich — managing a CHF 1.2 million budget, a team of data scientists, and workstreams spanning carbon removal, air travel, catering, and energy procurement. I developed the concept for what would be the world's first global university buyers club for durable CDR.

My prior roles include economist positions at the **World Bank** (Washington D.C.) and the **Inter-American Development Bank**, where I worked on energy, trade, competitiveness, fiscal decentralization, and extractive industry governance across more than 25 countries. At the **Federal Institute for Geosciences and Natural Resources (BGR, Germany)**, I led financial modelling of US$6.5 billion in climate-critical mineral procurement.

**Current research themes include:**
- Causal identification of barriers to public R&D funding uptake, using a 2×2 factorial RCT with AI-assisted interventions (Forschungszulage, Germany)
- Randomized evaluation of sustainable laboratory practices and their impact on energy use and researcher behaviour
- Green innovation policy and the returns to R&D tax credits: experimental IV evidence
- Transition risks for fossil-fuel-dependent nations: economic complexity and diversification pathways

My methods include **field experiments and RCTs**, econometrics, economic complexity analysis, and financial modelling.

I founded the [**d\carb Future Economy Forum**](https://www.decarb.world) in 2020 — a Berlin-based climate policy forum with global reach, convening leading economists, practitioners, and policymakers around durable CDR, green competitiveness, and the political economy of net zero. I organised the *Beyond Carbon: Debating the Future* public lecture series at UZH in Spring 2026.

I regularly lecture at **HEC Paris**, **ETH Zurich**, and the **Hertie School Berlin**, and have supervised MSc dissertations at Oxford and LSE. I welcome inquiries from prospective collaborators and supervisees.
